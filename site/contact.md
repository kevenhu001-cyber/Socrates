# Contact — Socrates

> Talk with us.

We answer every thoughtful note we receive. Partnership ideas, education research collaborations, research questions, and questions from people who use Socrates are all welcome.

## Email

- **General:** help@addtech.site
- **Privacy:** help@addtech.site (we will direct it internally)
- **Security disclosures:** see `/.well-known/security.txt`

## Response time

We aim to acknowledge within two business days. Substantive answers usually arrive within a week.

## What to include

If your note is about a partnership or a research collaboration, a one-paragraph overview of what you are building and what you would like from us is enough to start. We will ask follow-ups as needed.
