The story

A small team,
a large question.

2024

Research begins

We started by asking why existing AI tools made it so easy to receive an answer and so hard to know whether it had become yours.

2025

The first Socratic sessions

Early learners helped us shape a slower, more diagnostic conversation that could adjust when a prerequisite was missing.

2026

Open to more learners

Socrates grows into a product for students, self-learners, builders and anyone who wants to turn curiosity into a structure they can revisit.

What we believe

Principles over features.

These are the decisions we return to when the product gets complicated.

01

Depth over speed

Understanding cannot be rushed. We would rather help you learn one thing deeply than skim ten things superficially.

02

Teaching, not answering

A tutor’s job is not to remove every obstacle. It is to ask questions that lead to a mental model you can use without it.

03

Radical accessibility

Quality education should not require a credit card or an API key. Beagle lets anyone start the first conversation.

04

Honest signals

A wrong answer is useful information. The product should make uncertainty visible, then help you work with it.

Our learning method

Four habits that
make knowledge stick.

01 / FIRST PRINCIPLES

Begin at the root.

Define the terms, find the prerequisites, then build upward without pretending the gaps are not there.

02 / ACTIVE RECALL

Retrieve before rereading.

Questions interrupt passive recognition and ask your mind to bring the idea back into the room.

03 / ADAPTIVE PACING

Move at your speed.

Advance when the idea is stable. Slow down, change angle, or review foundations when it is not.

04 / ASSESSMENT

Practice the edge.

Structured exams show whether you can apply an idea after the conversation has ended.

We are not trying to make difficult ideas feel small. We are trying to make the path into them feel possible.
