About

Built by educators,
powered by AI

Socrates was created by a small, independent team who believe deep learning shouldn't require a classroom or a fortune. We are building an AI tutor that teaches the way great teachers do, by asking the right questions. The built-in Beagle AI model makes it instantly usable with no setup or API key required.

Socrates launched in early 2025 as a research project exploring whether modern language models could deliver genuine Socratic teaching, not just answering questions but guiding learners to construct understanding through structured dialogue, diagnostic checks, and adaptive pacing.

After months of testing with students across mathematics, programming, philosophy, and the sciences, we opened Socrates to the public. The response confirmed what we suspected: learners craved a tutor that challenged them to think, rather than one that simply handed out answers.

Today Socrates is used by learners in over 40 countries. Our team is a distributed group of five engineers, educators, and researchers based across China and North America. We are wholly owned and operated by Topodrive, based in Xi'an, China.

What we believe

01
Depth over speed
Understanding cannot be rushed. Every concept deserves to be taught from the ground up, layer by layer. We'd rather you learn one thing deeply than skim ten things superficially.
02
Teaching, not answering
A tutor's job is not to provide answers — it's to ask questions that lead to understanding. Every diagnostic check builds a mental model, not a transcript.
03
Radical accessibility
Quality education should not require a credit card or even an API key. The built-in Beagle AI model lets anyone start learning instantly. Free forever.

Our approach

01
First principles
Every lesson begins with foundations. Socrates identifies prerequisite concepts, defines terms clearly, and builds upward — never assuming prior knowledge it hasn't verified.
02
Active recall
Socrates interleaves explanation with diagnostic questions at natural intervals, forcing your brain to retrieve and apply information before it fades.
03
Adaptive pacing
Mastered a concept? Socrates advances. Struggling? It adapts with alternative explanations and foundational review, all in real time.
04
Exam & assessment
Test your knowledge with structured exams on any topic. Set difficulty and question types, answer at your own pace, submit for grading, and track results over time.
