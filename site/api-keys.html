How Socrates uses your keys

A short note on what we send and what we do not.

Your key is sent only to the provider you selected, and only with the prompts that arise from your sessions. We do not proxy traffic through our servers, do not log your prompts, and do not include any identifier that ties the request back to you beyond what your provider already sees.

Removing a key stops Socrates from using it on the next turn. Past turns that already used the key are preserved.
