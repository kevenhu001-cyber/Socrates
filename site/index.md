## Follow the question

Socrates doesn’t hand you the answer. It asks the next question, until the explanation is yours.

Start with what you think. Follow the question a little further. Keep the connection that makes it click.

### Illustrative session: Earth science

This is an illustrative session, not a real learner’s transcript.

1. **Your question:** Why is it hot in summer? **You:** ~~Because Earth is closer to the Sun.~~ (later revised)
2. **Socrates asks:** If that were true, what season would Australia have in July? **You:** Summer too… but it’s winter there. So distance can’t be it.
3. **Socrates asks:** Then what is different between the two halves of Earth in July? **You:** The tilt. The north leans toward the Sun, so its light arrives more directly.
4. **Socrates asks:** Can you say it to a friend in one sentence? **You:** Seasons come from Earth’s tilt: it changes how directly, and how long, sunlight reaches each half.

What you keep: seasons → axial tilt → sun angle and day length. Distance is crossed out.

## A tutor that asks before it answers

Start with what you think. Follow the question a little further. Keep the connection that makes it click.

### Illustrative session: probability

This is a non-interactive illustration, not a live conversation.

- **Learner:** Why isn’t a positive test the whole story?
- **Socrates:** If 1 in 100 people has the condition, how many is that in a group of 1,000?
- **Learner:** Ten. Some of the other 990 might test positive, too.
- **Socrates:** Exactly. A positive result needs context: the base rate and the false positives.

**A connection to keep:** Base rate → False positives.

Next time, try explaining this connection without looking at the conversation.

## One tutor, five ways to learn

Move between modes as an idea takes shape: talk it through, test it, connect it and come back to it. The product views below are illustrations.

- **[Dialogue](https://topodrive.top/product#dialogue):** think out loud, then go one step further. Socrates asks what you already know and offers the next question instead of the whole answer.
- **[Recall](https://topodrive.top/product#recall):** turn a good moment from a conversation into a card, then retrieve it in your own words a few days later.
- **[Knowledge map](https://topodrive.top/product#knowledge-map):** concepts you work through become points on a map, linked by the questions that joined them.
- **[Exam mode](https://topodrive.top/product#exam):** generate a practice exam on any topic, choose the difficulty and number of questions, then submit for grading.
- **[Mistake book](https://topodrive.top/product#mistake-book):** questions you miss in quizzes and practice are collected in one place, ready to redo until they hold.

## Inside a learning session

### Keep a good question going

An answer can be a beginning. Work through the part you cannot explain yet, then give yourself a reason to return.

1. [Put it in your own words](https://topodrive.top/product#dialogue). Socrates starts from what you already think and asks the next question, not the whole answer.
2. [Find the connection](https://topodrive.top/product#knowledge-map). Ideas you work through become points on your knowledge map, linked by the questions that joined them.
3. [Come back to it](https://topodrive.top/product#recall). Turn a good moment into a recall card, then answer it in your own words a few days later.
