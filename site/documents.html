How the library is organised

Three categories, one purpose.

Documents are sorted into guides, methods, and practice notes. Guides explain an idea. Methods describe a way of working. Practice notes describe what we have observed across many sessions.

Guides

Read once, return often

For ideas worth a slow first read.

Guides introduce a concept and give you enough context to ask the next question. They are written to be useful even if you only have ten minutes.

Methods

A way of working

For the practice of understanding.

Methods describe small habits: how to begin, how to test, how to recall, how to revise. They are most useful when paired with an actual subject you are studying.

Practice notes

What we observed

For the texture of real sessions.

Practice notes are short, observational pieces about how learning actually happens inside the tutor. They are the closest thing we have to field notes.
