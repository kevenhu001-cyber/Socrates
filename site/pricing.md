## What every plan includes

The same calm tutor, with more room to use it.

- **Teaching** — Socratic dialogue with streaming responses. LaTeX, code, and diagrams render inline.
- **Recall** — A small prompt queue that returns ideas on schedule. Recall history is private to your account.
- **Models** — Choose per session. Bring your own provider key from the API Keys page if you prefer.
- **Privacy** — Sessions are private. We do not train on them. Export or delete any session at any time.

## Choosing a plan

Most people do not need to think about this for long.

1. **How often will you open a session in a week?** One or two sessions → Free or Riemann. Daily → Descartes. Full-time → Euclid.
2. **How long do your sessions tend to run?** Short and focused → lower plans. Walk through entire chapters → more headroom.
3. **Will you use exam mode or web search?** Those live in Descartes and Euclid.

## Frequently asked

- **Cancellation:** Cancel any time from your account page. No retention calls.
- **Switching plans:** Move up or down from the account page. Upgrades take effect immediately and we pro-rate the unused portion.
- **Refunds:** Cancel within fourteen days of a first paid plan and write to us for a refund.
- **Students:** Send a note from your academic address to `help@addtech.site` with a course name and your institution, and we will send a discount code.
- **Invoices & VAT:** Add billing details from the account page. EU customers are billed with VAT applied where required.
- **Team plans:** Not yet as a self-serve plan. We work with research groups, classes, and small companies on a per-team basis — use the [contact](https://topodrive.top/contact) page.
