The Method

Three acts of a
single question

I
ἀπορία — aporia

The Impasse

Every dialogue begins in honest darkness. Socrates asks what you truly know — and shows you the edge of it, without judgment.

II
ἔλεγχος — elenchus

The Inquiry

Question by question, the fog scatters. Each answer you give becomes the ground for the next step — reasoning built by your own hands.

III
μαιευτική — maieutics

The Birth

The idea arrives whole — and it is yours, because you delivered it. Knowledge born this way is never forgotten.
