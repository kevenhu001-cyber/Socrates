Common questions

The practical details.

See plans

Is Socrates free?

Yes. The Free tier is permanently free, with no card and no trial clock. Paid tiers unlock more capacity and tools.

Does Socrates train on my conversations?

No. We do not train on user conversations. Every session can be exported or deleted from the account page.

Can I bring my own API key?

Yes. The Free tier and every paid tier support bringing your own key for any supported provider.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel any time from your account page. No retention calls.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel within fourteen days of a first paid plan and write to us for a refund.

Does Socrates support invoices and VAT?

Yes. Add billing details from the account page and EU customers are billed with VAT applied where required.
