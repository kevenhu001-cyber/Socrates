# Announcements — Socrates

> What we shipped recently.

Socrates releases in small, careful steps. Each change has a short note here. The full archive is available from the announcement index below.

## Recent

- **May 2026** — Follow-up prompts at the seam of an explanation. The tutor now waits for the learner to finish before offering a next-step prompt.
- **April 2026** — Multi-model per session. Choose a fast model for clarification, a deeper model for a long thread.
- **March 2026** — Knowledge map v2. The map now surfaces relationships from earlier sessions, not just the current one.
- **February 2026** — Mistake book. Save the moments where your explanation showed a seam; revisit them on a schedule.
- **January 2026** — Better BYOK. Bring your own key for any supported provider.
- **December 2025** — Recall queue refresh. Easier scheduling, more control over cadence.
