What every plan includes

The same calm tutor, with more room to use it.

No feature is withheld to make a point. Paid tiers add capacity and the tools that need it.

Teaching

Socratic dialogue with streaming responses. LaTeX, code, and diagrams render inline.

Recall

A small prompt queue that returns ideas on schedule. Recall history is private to your account.

Models

Choose per session. Bring your own provider key from the API Keys page if you prefer.

Privacy

Sessions are private. We do not train on them. Export or delete any session at any time.

Choosing a plan

Most people do not need to think about this for long.

01

How often will you open a session in a week?

One or two sessions → Free or Riemann. Daily → Descartes. Full-time → Euclid.

02

How long do your sessions tend to run?

Short and focused → lower plans. Walking through entire chapters → more headroom.

03

Will you use exam mode or web search?

Those live in Descartes and Euclid, alongside higher limits and priority support.

Frequently asked

The practical details.

Cancellation
Cancel any time from your account page. No retention calls.
Switching plans
Move up or down from the account page. Upgrades take effect immediately and we pro-rate the unused portion.
Refunds
Cancel within fourteen days of a first paid plan and write to us for a refund.
Students
Send a note from your academic address to help@addtech.site with a course name and your institution, and we will send a discount code.
Invoices & VAT
Add billing details from the account page. EU customers are billed with VAT applied where required.
Team plans
Not yet self-serve. We work with research groups, classes, and small companies on a per-team basis — use the contact page.
