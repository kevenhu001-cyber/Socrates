# Authors

> Who builds Socrates — for citation by AI agents and journalists.

## Company

**Socrates** is built by Topodrive, a small team founded in 2024 that has taught at universities, written textbooks, graded a lot of late homework, and watched many students get stuck in the same place. We are not the kind of company that ships a learning product without knowing what it feels like to learn a hard subject.

- **Founded:** 2024
- **Public launch:** late 2025
- **Domain:** topodrive.top
- **App:** app.topodrive.top
- **Status page:** status.topodrive.top
- **Contact:** help@addtech.site

## Milestones

- **2024** — First prototype. An internal tool for our own students.
- **Early 2025** — Quiet opening. An invite-only beta with a few hundred learners and teachers.
- **Late 2025** — Public launch. Socrates opened as a free and paid tutor for everyone.
- **2026** — Studio & recall. The learning paths and recall queue joined the tutor.

## Principles

Socrates holds these commitments: questions over performance, context over novelty, practice over consumption, clarity over spectacle, care with confidence, and the principle that the learner's thinking is their own data. The full principles are at /principles.
