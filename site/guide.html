01
The first move

Start with the question you actually have.

Do not wait until the prompt sounds academic. “Why does this work?”, “what am I missing?”, and “show me a smaller example” are all good places to begin. Socrates uses your wording to find a teachable first step.

The structure

Socrates defines the terms, checks prerequisites, and layers examples on top. If a concept is shaky, it goes sideways before it moves forward. That detour is the point.

04
The practice

Answer before you look it up.

When Socrates asks for a prediction, explanation, or example, pause. A rough answer gives the system more signal than a perfect copy-paste, and gives you a clearer place to improve.
